# DailyHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

DailyHub is a local-first personal productivity app. This policy describes
what data the app handles.

## Data stored on your device

Tasks, categories, workouts, projects, settings, and reminders you create
are stored in a local database on your device and never leave it unless
you enable one of the optional features below.

## Optional Google Drive backup

If you sign in with Google and grant Drive access, the app uploads an
encrypted-by-transport backup of your data to your own Google Drive
(`drive.appdata` application data folder, invisible in Drive UI). Sync is
manual or automatic only after sign-in. Signing out stops all uploads;
you can delete the backup by removing the app's Drive data in your
Google account settings.

## Optional managed account (Supabase)

If you create a DailyHub account, your profile name, username, avatar,
friend connections, shared spaces, and their contents are stored on the
app's Supabase backend to provide social and sharing features. You can
delete your account data by signing out and requesting deletion at the
support email below.

## Analytics, ads, tracking

None. The app contains no analytics SDKs, no ads, and no cross-app
trackers. Crash behavior is not collected.
