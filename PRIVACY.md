# DailyHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

DailyHub stores tasks, task notes, subtasks, categories, standalone notes and
settings locally on your device. It has no accounts, cloud sync, analytics,
advertising, social features or backend. This version does not upload your
tasks or notes.

## Network access

Automatic update checks are enabled by default and can be disabled in Settings.
They contact GitHub's public latest-release endpoint for Moltrax8/DailyHub when
the app opens or returns to the foreground, throttled to once per 15 minutes.
A manual check is also available. APKs are downloaded from GitHub only after
you choose to download/install. GitHub receives normal connection information
such as your IP address under its own privacy policy. No personal content is sent.

Privacy, terms and releases links open your external browser only when tapped.
The app contains no other network features.

## Permissions and storage

Notifications and alarms support local task reminders. Install-package permission
supports the system update installer; updates are signature-checked and require
your consent. Uninstalling the app deletes its local data. Android device transfer
may copy local data according to the system's transfer settings; the app database
is excluded from Android cloud backup. Keep this in mind before uninstalling.

Upgrading from older versions removes obsolete workout and shared-project caches.
This does not delete data already stored by previous versions in external services;
manage any such copies directly with those providers.

For questions, open an issue at https://github.com/Moltrax8/DailyHub/issues.
