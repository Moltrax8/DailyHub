# Usage and quality controls

**Do** preserve the relative spacing of the bars, stem and diamond. Use the brand mark at least 24 px for recognizable colored detail; use monochrome symbol for smaller UI surfaces. Keep 12% or more clearspace around logos. Preserve hue relationships when creating new status badges.

**Don't** replace the symbol with a notification bell or a calendar; those are contextual pictograms, not brand identifiers. Don't use the JPEG-like concept board as a production asset. Don't rely solely on red/green to identify statuses; include accessible text labels.

**Notification usage:** `unread` is neutral unread state; `deadline` signals an approaching due date; `reminder` is a scheduled prompt; `activity` is routine update; `friend-request` is social action; `update` is new software version; `completed` marks success.

**Accessibility:** Most pictograms need an adjacent visible label or an `aria-label` with the notification type. Do not assume color communicates the complete state.
